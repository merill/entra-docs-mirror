| June 2026 | Roles | Updated [AI Administrator](permissions-reference#ai-administrator) role and [AI Reader](permissions-reference#ai-reader) roles. |

| March 2026 | Roles | Added [Entra Backup Administrator](permissions-reference#entra-backup-administrator) and [Entra Backup Reader](permissions-reference#entra-backup-reader) roles. |
| March 2026 | Roles | Added [Tenant Governance Administrator](permissions-reference#tenant-governance-administrator), [Tenant Governance Reader](permissions-reference#tenant-governance-reader), [Tenant Governance Relationship Administrator](permissions-reference#tenant-governance-relationship-administrator), and [Tenant Governance Relationship Reader](permissions-reference#tenant-governance-relationship-reader) roles. |
